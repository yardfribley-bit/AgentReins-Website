Website analytics

To understand launch traffic and keep this website reliable, we record time, page or download architecture, referrer, IP address, user agent, device, screen size, and language. First-party anonymous visitor and session identifiers distinguish repeat visits from launch previews. We also record scroll milestones, viewed product sections, 30-second engagement, outbound link clicks, and download intent. We derive approximate country, region, city, ASN, and network owner from the IP address. This is an estimate, not a precise address or identity. We do not collect form input, passwords, advertising identifiers, or cross-site browsing history.

Purpose and retention

Records support aggregate launch analytics, product improvement, abuse prevention, and operational security. Raw records and IP geolocation results are retained for up to 30 days; aggregate counts may be retained longer.

AgentReins application

The AgentReins macOS application is local-first. Agent evidence remains on the user's device unless the user explicitly configures an external analysis provider.

Your choices

You can block analytics using browser privacy controls without losing website access. To request access or deletion, email yardfribley@gmail.com or open a GitHub issue.
