Website analytics

To understand launch traffic and keep this website reliable, we record a limited visit or download event: time, requested page or download architecture, referring page, IP address, browser, operating system, device category, screen size, and language. We do not collect form input, passwords, cookies, advertising identifiers, or cross-site browsing history.

Purpose and retention

These records are used for aggregate launch analytics, abuse prevention, and operational security. Raw records are retained for 30 days and then deleted. Aggregate counts may be retained longer.

AgentReins application

The AgentReins macOS application is local-first. Its agent evidence remains on the user's device unless the user explicitly configures an external analysis provider.

Your choices

You can block the analytics request using browser privacy controls without losing access to the website. To request access or deletion, open an issue in the AgentReins GitHub repository.
